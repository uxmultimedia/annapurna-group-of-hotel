change `.webp`, `.jpg`, or `.png` extensions.

The six hotel folders are:

- `hotels/mohit-regency/`
- `hotels/kamla-regency/`
- `hotels/divine-casa/`
- `hotels/ct-palace/`

Place these files inside every hotel folder:

- `hero.webp`
- `overview.webp`

- `banquet.webp`
- `cta.webp`

- `lobby.webp`
- `rooms.webp`

## Recommended export sizes

- Full-width hero and CTA images: `1920 × 1080`
- Editorial portrait images: `1200 × 1500`
- Hotel and room cards: `1200 × 900`
- Gallery images: at least `1400px` on the long edge
- Logo: transparent PNG, approximately `440 × 208`

Use compressed WebP files wherever `.webp` is specified.
